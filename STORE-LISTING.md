```
Settle prepares you for the Life in the UK test — the exam you must pass for
indefinite leave to remain or British citizenship.

Everything is included in one payment. No subscription, no adverts, no locked
chapters, and no internet connection needed.

WHAT YOU GET

• 280 practice questions across all five chapters of the syllabus, each with a
  written explanation of why the answer is right
• Timed mock tests — 24 questions in 45 minutes, marked at 18 out of 24, run
  exactly like the real thing with flagged questions and a review grid
• 23 study sections covering values, the four nations, history from Stonehenge
  to the present day, society, and how government and the law work
• 93 flashcards for the dates, names and numbers that catch people out
• Listening and speaking practice at B1 level, the English standard required
  alongside the test
• A plain-English guide to booking, ID requirements, fees, test day, retakes,
  and what happens after you pass

HOW IT HELPS YOU PASS

Questions are shuffled every time, so you learn the material instead of
memorising answer positions. The app tracks which questions you get wrong and
lets you drill only those. Mastery is shown per chapter, so you can see which
part of the syllabus is letting you down before the exam does.

Set your test date and the home screen counts down and tells you roughly how
many questions a day will keep you on pace.

BUILT FOR REAL STUDY

• Works completely offline — on the bus, at work, anywhere
• Any question or study section can be read aloud, which helps if English is
  your second language
• Dark mode and four text sizes
• Progress is saved on your device and never leaves it

IMPORTANT

Settle is an independent study app. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or
connected to the Home Office or any UK government department. The official
source of test material is the Home Office handbook, Life in the United Kingdom:
A Guide for New Residents. Use this app alongside the handbook.

Fees, rules and requirements change. Always confirm current details on GOV.UK
before booking your test or submitting an application.
```

### Capturing screenshots

Open the app on a phone, or in Chrome DevTools device mode set to 1080×2400,
and capture these six:

1. **Home** — the Route 24 bus strip with some progress showing
2. **A mock test question** mid-test, timer visible
3. **Results** — the score card with the per-chapter breakdown
4. **A study section** — history, showing the "Remember these" box
5. **Flashcards** — a date card
6. **English B1 listening** — a passage with its questions

Put some progress in first; empty screens sell badly. Do not add promotional
text over screenshots that implies official status.
